Common Questions

Everything
You Need to Know.

Is Kashmir safe to visit right now?
+
Yes — Kashmir's tourist areas (Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam) are extremely safe and welcoming. Thousands visit every month. Our team is local and we monitor conditions in real time. We'd never send you somewhere we wouldn't go ourselves — our families live here.
What's the best time to visit Kashmir?
+
April–June is peak season with blooming gardens and pleasant weather. July–September brings lush green valleys. December–February is magical for snow lovers in Gulmarg. Each season offers something unique — we'll recommend the best time for what you want to experience.
Can I customise the packages?
+
Absolutely. Every package is a starting point. Add nights, combine destinations, upgrade your houseboat, include honeymooner extras, or request a group-specific plan. WhatsApp us and we'll build something just for you — usually within 2 hours.
How does the payment work?
+
Just 40% advance via UPI to confirm your slot. The remaining 60% is due 7 days before your departure. We accept all major UPI apps, bank transfers, and cards. Receipts provided immediately for every payment.
What's your cancellation policy?
+
Cancel 10+ days before: full refund of advance. Cancel 7–10 days before: 50% refund. Under 7 days: no refund but we'll credit it toward your next trip. Date changes (10+ days prior) are always free — we simply rebook everything for you.
Do you handle solo travellers?
+
Yes, and we love solo travellers. We match them with cosy guesthouses, private guides, and our local contacts so you always feel connected. Many of our solo guests become regulars who come back with friends. Kashmir is safe and extraordinary for solo travel.
